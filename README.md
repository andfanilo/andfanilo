## :cherry_blossom: Ship Decisions, Not Tools

I'm an AI Strategist with a Solutions Architect background, on a quest to help teams turn AI ambitions into business decisions. I've spent 12+ years framing business cases for data & AI, then shaping the results into artifacts a decision-maker can open and audit: benchmarks, cost models, reports, decks, as Streamlit apps, JS websites, After Effects videos or Excel spreadsheets.

My work at [Magellan](https://www.magellangroup.eu/) is an even mix of building and advising on AI strategy for a CCaaS product. Before an AI feature lands on a roadmap, I've usually built a rough version myself. That's how I know where it breaks and what it costs, not just the pitch. Lately that means benchmarking speech-to-text models and drafting cost-per-audio-minute models to feed a make-or-buy recommendation.

I'm also a university lecturer in MLOps, Big Data & NoSQL, and a member of the [Streamlit Creators](https://discuss.streamlit.io/t/introducing-streamlit-creators/6207) Ambassador Program.
